Reswell is the surfer-to-surfer marketplace for buying and selling surfboards. Here is why it exists, and where it comes from.

Some boards move fine on Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist. Others sit for months. Someone out there might be looking for that same board, and they just aren’t in your town.

Reswell started from a simple idea: buying and selling surfboards doesn’t have to stop at local pickup. We wanted one dedicated place for boards and surf gear, where finding what you want is straightforward and sellers can reach buyers who are actually looking.

01

What it is

A nationwide marketplace where surfers message, agree on a price, and check out on Reswell. Shipping is built in when pickup isn’t the right fit.

02

How it started

Cofounders Hayden Garfield and David Kalt built the marketplace they thought should exist: focused on boards, not another general classifieds site.

03

Where we’re based

Santa Barbara, California. Even here, the board you want isn’t always around the corner. Reswell is for surfers nationwide.

We’re not another Facebook Marketplace. Those places are useful, and they’ll keep existing. For surfers who know what they want, and for people who buy and sell boards often, we wanted something more focused: ship the board, skip the meetup logistics, and build the exchange around trust.
